**Privacy Notice**
**Last updated: 2026-08-23**

This educational farming game is designed especially for children. We take privacy and safety seriously.

### We collect no personal information

- We do **not** ask for or collect any personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, or location data.
- There are **no accounts**, **no logins**, and **no user profiles**.
- The game does **not** use tracking cookies, advertising, analytics services, or any third-party tools that collect data.
- Nothing is sent to any server. The game runs entirely in your web browser.

### What is stored on your device

The game saves progress only on **your device** using the browser's local storage (or similar technology). This may include:

- Farm layout and buildings
- Crops, animals, and inventory
- Currency and unlocked features
- Completed missions and settings

This information stays on the device and is never uploaded or shared.

### How to clear or reset game data

You (or a parent/guardian) can erase all saved progress at any time:

1. Open the game's Settings menu.
2. Choose **Reset Game Data**.

You can also clear the data through your browser's settings (look for "Clear site data" or "Local storage" for this website).

### Children's privacy

This game is intended for children and follows a "no personal data" design. Because we do not collect personal information from anyone (including children under 13), we do not need parental consent under laws such as the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) or similar rules in other countries.

Parents and guardians remain in full control of the device and can clear the game data whenever they wish.

### No sharing or selling of data

We do not share, sell, or transfer any information because we do not collect any personal information.

### Changes to this notice

If we ever change how the game works in a way that affects privacy, we will update this notice and clearly show the new date at the top.
